# Fitted demos and public speech examples

`catalog.json` describes the guided demos and their completed evaluations. The per-contrast JSON files contain our fitted imputation, scaling, and linear-classifier coefficients, plus the pinned upstream encoder specification. They are generated by `python -m research.build_demos` after the frozen evaluation and robustness gates. Models are not fitted on the user's recordings.

`audio/*.flac` are short, lossless word/phone crops of public **LibriSpeech** recordings, **CC BY 4.0**. They are human speech, not generated reference voices. Credit: Vassil Panayotov, Guoguo Chen, Daniel Povey, Sanjeev Khudanpur; source readings from LibriVox volunteers. [Original corpus](https://www.openslr.org/12), [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Word/phone timings were generated with Montreal Forced Aligner: [Loren Lugosch](https://zenodo.org/records/2619474). Dataset packaging: [Kim Gilkey](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gilkeyio/librispeech-alignments). `attribution.json` gives every source utterance, speaker ID, split, and crop timestamp. Timings have not been manually certified. Clips are deterministically chosen for common words and speaker/word diversity, not for classifier correctness.

These fitted directions measure English category resemblance. They are not calibrated pronunciation grades, anatomical measurements, or validated Mandarin coaching. Large encoder weights download separately under their upstream licenses; full corpora, intermediate feature arrays, and personal recordings are not included here.
