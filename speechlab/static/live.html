What does the position mean?

The expanded map covers all 21 Mandarin initial consonants, final ng, and six vowels. Choose a sound family to show fewer reference regions; the coordinates stay fixed. The original nine-sound map is also available. Tone, glides, and the special “i” in shi are outside these categories.

Stops and affricates are brief: say a syllable and watch the trail, then press Freeze trace. The expanded model was fitted to 160 ms windows that can include adjacent sounds. It does not automatically isolate a consonant from the rest of a syllable.

References: THCHS-30 (Apache 2.0), via changelinglab’s audio package and anyspeech’s phone timings. The timing labels have not been independently verified. Reference words retain their natural surrounding sounds.
